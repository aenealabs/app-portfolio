# Ardent Systems — Vaultline Classic Support Renewal (fictional excerpt)

## Support Term
Support for Vaultline Classic is provided through November 30, 2026. This agreement renews automatically for an additional twelve (12) months unless Customer provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the current term.

## End of Life
Vaultline Classic reaches end of vendor support on March 31, 2027. After that date Ardent will provide no security patches. Support fees for any period after end of life are non-refundable.

## Fees
Support fees for a renewal term are payable in full in advance and increase by eight percent (8%).

## Data Export
On request, Ardent will provide a one-time bulk export of vault contents at a fee of $40,000.
