# Voltaic — Software License and Maintenance Agreement (fictional excerpt)

Covers: CircuitStudio Pro (named users) and HarnessCAD (named users)

## Term
The maintenance term ends December 15, 2026 and renews automatically for one (1) year terms unless Customer cancels by written notice no later than sixty (60) days before the renewal date.

## Fees and Adjustments
Maintenance fees for each renewal year may increase by up to six percent (6%).

## Named User Compliance
Voltaic may audit named-user counts once per year. Named users above the licensed quantity are billed as a true-up at list price plus fifteen percent (15%), retroactive to the start of the current term.

## Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on thirty (30) days written notice if the breach is not cured.
