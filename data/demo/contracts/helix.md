# Helix Engineering — SimuForge Token Subscription (fictional excerpt)

## Term and Renewal
Initial term: May 1, 2024 to April 30, 2027. The subscription renews automatically for twelve (12) months unless either party gives notice at least thirty (30) days before expiry.

## Token Pool
Customer purchases a pool of 70 solver tokens. When the pool is exhausted, jobs wait in queue; additional tokens may be purchased at the then-current rate.

## Price
Renewal pricing increases by seven percent (7%) per year.

## Data Retention
Helix retains simulation results for sixty (60) days after termination, after which they are deleted.
