# Keystone PLM — Enterprise Subscription Agreement (fictional excerpt)

Order Form KS-2024-0417 · Customer: Engineering IT · Product: PLM platform, concurrent engineer seats

## 1. Subscription Term and Renewal
1.1 The Subscription Term runs from February 1, 2024 through January 31, 2027.
1.2 The Subscription Term renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Fees
2.1 Annual fees are invoiced in advance. Fees for any renewal term increase by five percent (5%) over the fees for the prior term.
2.2 Seat reductions take effect only at renewal and require the notice in Section 1.2.

## 3. Usage and Compliance
3.1 Concurrent seats measure the peak number of simultaneous sessions. Customer may run a usage report at any time.
3.2 There is no true-up for concurrent seats; sessions above the entitlement are queued until a seat is free.

## 4. Data
4.1 On termination, Customer may export all data in documented open formats for ninety (90) days.
