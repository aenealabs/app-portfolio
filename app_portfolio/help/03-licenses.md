# Licenses and renewals

The Licenses page puts every renewal deadline in one place, with the money at stake.

## Notice deadlines

Most engineering software contracts renew automatically unless you give written notice a set
number of days before the renewal. The deadline is the renewal date minus that notice period,
both taken from the contract only after code has checked them.

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Passed | Too late to stop this renewal: plan for the next one |
| Due within 30 days | Act now |
| Within 90 days | Put it on the agenda |
| Later | Nothing to do yet |

A license with no contract on file, or a contract not read yet, has no deadline: it is shown
as unknown rather than guessed.

## Spare seats and savings

A license under 75% used has spare seats: those above the 90-day peak plus 10% headroom.
The saving is what those seats cost a year if you reduce them at renewal. Concurrent and
token licenses are measured by peak use; named licenses by active named users.

## True-up exposure

For named-user licenses, people above the entitlement are billed at the next audit or
renewal. The exposure is those users times the unit cost, plus any premium the contract
states (for example, list price plus 15%).

## Built-in increases

Contracts often raise the price at each renewal. The increase shown is this year's spend
times the checked percentage.
